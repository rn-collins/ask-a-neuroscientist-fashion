# Slide visual audit

Audited 8 September 2026. Scope: all seven Ask a Neuroscientist × Fashion platform kits.

## Governing decision

A cleared, claim-relevant real image is preferred. A sourced evidence graphic is used when it explains the claim more accurately than the available object photography. Editorial typography is reserved for the evidence-boundary close and the alternate YouTube thesis treatment. Protected link-only media is never reproduced. No generative imagery is used.

## Results

- 168 rendered PNG exports were regenerated and inspected structurally: 112 Instagram frames, 35 Pinterest pins, 14 YouTube thumbnails and seven vertical storyboards.
- Across the 147 individual Instagram and Pinterest slides, 59 use cleared real-image treatments, 74 use purpose-built evidence-graphic treatments and 14 use intentional editorial typography.
- Every Instagram and Pinterest record now declares `visualMode` and `visualRationale`.
- No carousel or Pinterest sequence repeats a real image internally. A cleared object can recur in a different platform treatment when the crop and editorial purpose change.
- Every non-storyboard export records its source, credit, rights field, alt text, visual mode and editorial rationale.

## Honest limitation

Packages AAN-F-004 (memory) and AAN-F-005 (Fashion Week load) each currently have only one cleared, locally reproducible real-image witness. Their other slides therefore use evidence graphics and typography rather than repeating that image or reproducing protected media. This is an intentional rights-and-meaning decision, not a claim that every slide has a different photograph.
