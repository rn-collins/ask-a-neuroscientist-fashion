## Publication rule

A future discovery is installed only when its work-level reuse or embed authority is explicit, its provenance is exact, it is non-AI, and it improves the specific argument or visual sequence. A relevant source is not automatically an appropriate image. Link-only and reserve records remain outside installed media until every gate is satisfied.
