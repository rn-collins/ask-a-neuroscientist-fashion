# AAN-F-006 — Seven vertical videos

Each is 35–55 seconds. Use RN voice, captions, source card and licensed/original sound only.

## 01 — The mute test
**Hook:** Mute a runway. What disappeared if the clothes are still there?
**Script:** Sound supplies timing, expectation and association. Play the same authorized BFC clip muted, then audible inside its embed. Describe what changes for you. Do not call your response universal.
**Boundary:** A video is not the in-room acoustic scene.
**Bridge:** Full listening room in the gallery.

## 02 — Entrainment is not mind control
**Hook:** Neuroscience uses a word that sounds far more sinister than it is.
**Script:** Entrainment means activity or movement aligning with temporal structure. Beat research finds auditory and motor systems supporting prediction. That is not hypnosis, obedience or lost agency.
**Source:** Patel & Iversen 2014; Merchant et al. 2015.

## 03 — The runway is an auditory scene
**Hook:** You never hear “the soundtrack” alone.
**Script:** Music, shoes, applause, voices and room reflections arrive together. Your auditory system groups the mixture. Microphones and editors group it again for viewers.
**Source:** Trainor 2015.

## 04 — Why the drop works—sometimes
**Hook:** A drop feels powerful because a listener predicted something first.
**Script:** Learned musical regularities generate expectation. Delay and violation can redirect attention and arousal. Familiarity and culture change the prediction; there is no universal formula.
**Source:** Di Liberto et al. 2020.

## 05 — Music edits the garment
**Hook:** The soundtrack can change the sentence an outfit seems to finish.
**Script:** Audio and image are integrated, especially when timing or content feels congruent. That changes interpretation, not the garment’s physical properties.
**Source:** Su 2014.

## 06 — A track can carry a life
**Hook:** Sometimes the most powerful thing on a runway is your own history.
**Script:** Familiar music can cue autobiographical memory and emotion. The memory is reconstructive and personal; another listener may have none.
**Source:** Janata 2009.

## 07 — You cannot hear dose through a video
**Hook:** “That show sounded loud” is not a sound measurement.
**Script:** Dose depends on level, duration, spectrum, distance and room acoustics. A published recording cannot establish exposure at one seat or backstage.
**Source:** WHO environmental-noise guideline.

End card for all: **Powerful ≠ universal. Watch the full exhibition.**
