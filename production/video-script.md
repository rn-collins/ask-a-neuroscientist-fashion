# Film — The Study Everyone Quotes

**OPEN (0:00–0:30)**  “A lab coat makes you smarter” is a better headline than it is a scientific conclusion.

**OBJECT (0:30–2:30)**  Read the Maison Léoty corset as construction, bodily contact and social silhouette. Do not imply a neural effect.

**CLAIM (2:30–6:00)**  Explain the 2012 manipulation, task and reported result. On-screen DOI.

**REPLICATION (6:00–10:00)**  Explain Burns, Fox, Greenstein and Montgomery’s preregistered direct replication and null result. No verdict-by-vibes.

**BOUNDARY (10:00–14:00)**  Separate garment, symbolic label, observer, task and outcome. State what remains unresolved.

**CLOSE**  “Before you ask what clothing does to the brain, ask what changed—and what anyone actually measured.”
