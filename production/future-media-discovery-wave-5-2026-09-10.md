## Rights and presentation decisions

- Hanada is an exact Frontiers article under CC BY 4.0. It is linked as a complete study; its apparatus and plots are not repurposed as fashion imagery.
- Engeln and Zola is available through PMC but carries an exclusive-license notice rather than a current Creative Commons grant. It remains link-only, and its exploratory self-report results do not establish causality.
- Ansari, Shepard and Gottfried is treated as link-only. Public availability through PMC is not converted into an inferred reuse license.
- Kim et al. is CC BY-NC 4.0. It is linked, not reproduced, because noncommercial status must not be assumed for a public RN publication.

## Standing publication rule

A future discovery enters the installed visual sequence only when its work-level reuse or embed authority is explicit, its provenance is exact, it is demonstrably non-AI, and it improves the specific argument or visual sequence. Relevant scholarship can improve a gallery as a resource without becoming decorative media.
