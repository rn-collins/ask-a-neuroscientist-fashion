# AAN-F-001 — Clothes and Cognition

## Governing question
Can what we wear change attention, abstraction, physiology or conduct—and how strong is the evidence?

## Publication sequence
1. The claim: Adam & Galinsky (2012).
2. The replication: Burns, Fox, Greenstein & Montgomery (2019).
3. The boundary: Horton, Adam & Galinsky (2023).
4. The object: Maison Léoty corset, Met 82433, CC0.
5. The invitation: fit the sentence to the evidence.

All outputs must preserve DOI/source links, object credit, non-color evidence labels, captions/transcripts, and “what this does not prove.”
