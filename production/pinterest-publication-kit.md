# Ask a Neuroscientist × Fashion — Pinterest publication kit

These are finished Pin copy and art-direction masters. Use only the named Public Domain/CC0 object or RN-owned photography. Never paste a paper figure, runway still or third-party photograph into a Pin without a separate permission record.

## AAN-F-001 — Clothes and Cognition
- **Pin title:** Can Clothes Actually Change How You Think?
- **Overlay:** THE CLAIM / THE REPLICATION / THE LIMIT
- **Description:** The famous lab-coat finding is compelling—and disputed. Walk through the original claim, the preregistered replication and the evidence boundary in The Dressed Nervous System.
- **Image:** Maison Léoty, *Corset*, 1891, The Met, C.I.45.27a, b, Public Domain/CC0.

## AAN-F-002 — Outfit as Armor
- **Pin title:** Why Does the Right Outfit Feel Like Armor?
- **Overlay:** PROTECTION IS A PATHWAY, NOT A DIAGNOSIS
- **Description:** Pressure, ritual, memory and other people’s responses can all contribute to an “armor” outfit. Map the pathway without pretending the metaphor is a brain scan.
- **Image:** French armor object identified in the package rights ledger, The Met, Public Domain/CC0.

## AAN-F-003 — The Sensory Textile Room
- **Pin title:** Your Skin Does Not Read the Fiber Label
- **Overlay:** FIBER ≠ FEEL
- **Description:** Texture is an encounter among material, construction, body site, duration, temperature, movement and expectation. Compare two garments in the no-storage textile map.
- **Image:** *Riding coat*, ca. 1760, The Met, 1976.147.1, Public Domain/CC0.

## AAN-F-004 — The Memory Wardrobe
- **Pin title:** Why Can Old Clothes Feel Like They Hold a Person?
- **Overlay:** VIVIDNESS IS NOT VERIFICATION
- **Description:** A garment can cue autobiographical memory through sight, touch, smell, movement and social history. The memory is meaningful—and reconstructive.
- **Image:** Package 004 lead object recorded in its rights ledger, The Met, Public Domain/CC0.

## AAN-F-005 — The Fashion Week Nervous System
- **Pin title:** Fashion Week Is Not One Stimulus
- **Overlay:** CLOCK / LIGHT / SOUND / CROWD / BODY / EVALUATION
- **Description:** Fashion Week can be exhilarating and draining in the same day. Map the conditions without inventing a special Fashion Week brain.
- **Image:** RN-owned field photograph after release review, or use the official BFC video as link-only. Do not extract a still.

## AAN-F-006 — The Listening Room
- **Pin title:** Why Are Runway Soundtracks So Powerful?
- **Overlay:** POWERFUL ≠ UNIVERSAL
- **Description:** A runway soundtrack organizes an auditory scene, supplies temporal predictions and meets personal memory. Entrainment is not mind control.
- **Image:** Maelzel *Metronome*, ca. 1821, The Met, 1979.1.2, Public Domain/CC0.

## AAN-F-007 — The Role Room
- **Pin title:** A Uniform Is Read Before Its Wearer Speaks
- **Overlay:** SIGNAL ≠ PROOF
- **Description:** Uniforms can make a role legible and organize social inference. They cannot verify competence, authority, trust or character.
- **Image:** *Uniform*, late 19th century, The Met, 1979.152.54a–h, Public Domain/CC0.
