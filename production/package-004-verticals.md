# AAN-F-004 — Seven vertical scripts

## 01 — The door (35 seconds)
HOOK: “Your old jacket does not contain a memory.”
BODY: “But its cuff, weight or smell may overlap with cues encoded during an event. That can help autobiographical memory reconstruct a scene. Reconstruct—not replay.”
BOUNDARY CARD: Vivid ≠ verified.
BRIDGE: “The full memory wardrobe is linked.”

## 02 — The perfume in the collar (40 seconds)
HOOK: “Sometimes it is not the dress. It is what the dress still smells like.”
BODY: “Odor-cue studies find unusually emotional autobiographical recall. That makes scent a plausible part of a garment memory, but not proof the remembered episode is accurate.”
SOURCE: Herz & Schooler (2002).

## 03 — The song beside the outfit (40 seconds)
HOOK: “Why can one song bring back your whole going-out uniform?”
BODY: “Familiar music can cue autobiographical memories and emotion. The outfit and song may be converging cues. There is no evidence for one fashion-nostalgia center.”
SOURCE: Janata (2009).

## 04 — Three nostalgias (45 seconds)
HOOK: “Loving Y2K fashion does not mean you remember Y2K.”
BODY: “Your lived past is autobiographical nostalgia. A group’s story is collective nostalgia. A revival may simply be retro style, historical curiosity or marketing. Related does not mean identical.”

## 05 — A scan cannot authenticate memory (35 seconds)
HOOK: “A glowing brain image cannot prove that your memory happened exactly that way.”
BODY: “Nostalgia studies implicate distributed memory, self, emotion and reward systems. A scan cannot read out the historical truth of an episode.”

## 06 — The museum dress (40 seconds)
HOOK: “This dress has two histories: the documented one and the one you bring to it.”
BODY: “The record can establish maker, date, material and provenance. Your association may be genuine, but it belongs in a different evidence column.”

## 07 — Map the garment (50 seconds)
HOOK: “Try this before saying an outfit transported you.”
BODY: “Name the object. Name the cue: sight, touch, smell, movement, music or room. Write what came to mind. Then separately list what can be checked—a date, label, photograph or another account.”
CTA: “Use the private Memory Garment Field Map. Nothing is stored.”
