# AAN-F-004 — Carousel copy

## Carousel A: The garment is a door
1. **The garment is not the memory. It can be the door.**
2. Clothing gathers visual, tactile, movement, smell and social cues.
3. A cue overlapping with an encoded event can support retrieval.
4. Autobiographical memory reconstructs; it does not replay.
5. Emotion can make recall vivid without making every detail accurate.
6. Separate **remembered experience** from **corroborated record**.
7. Map one garment privately. Nothing is stored.

Image direction: Met CC0 garment details only; captions and object records on every object slide.

## Carousel B: Which nostalgia do you mean?
1. **Not every retro feeling is your memory.**
2. Autobiographical: a past you personally lived.
3. Collective: a group story about a shared past.
4. Historical: knowledge of a period and its objects.
5. Retro marketing: familiar codes used to shape attention or desire.
6. One look can activate several—but evidence for one cannot prove another.
7. Ask: *What did I live, what did I learn, and what is being sold?*
